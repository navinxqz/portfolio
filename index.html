Who I Am

Hi I’m Navin, a third-year undergraduate Computer Science and Engineering student at American International Univeristy-Bangladesh (AIUB), driven by a passion for technology, creativity and continuous learning. Over the past two years, I’ve been building my skills in software development, focusing on C# and .NET frameworks, while also exploring web development. My journey is a blend of hands-on projects, problem-solving and a desire to make things that are both functional and accessible. I completed my Intermediate studies at the prestigious Notre Dame College, which played a significant role in shaping my dedication and curiosity in the field of science and technology.

In addition to coding, I’m a digital artist and enjoy expressing my creativity in both design and illustration. My interests extend to web development as well, where I’m building and learning to create dynamic webpages, UI interactions. Each of my projects reflect my curiosity and love for creative problem-solving, from developing user-friendly applications to creating digital art.

My aim is to contribute to open-source projects, that solve real-world problems and make some positive impact 🌱 I strive to make each day a step forward in becoming someone I can be proud of, constantly driven by a belief in gratitude and the pursuit of knowledge.
